<CheatRule label="The single most important idea">
  Validation is the discipline of making sure the score you computed survives contact with production. <Highlight>A model can score 99% on your laptop and 75% in deployment</Highlight> — not because the model is bad, but because the evaluation wasn't honest.
</CheatRule>

Pages 1–5 (foundations, classification, regression, probability, clustering) belong to Part 4; **Page 6 — Model validation** is the focus of this post and the reason the cheat sheet lives here too.*

<CheatRule label="Final exam traps" accent="red">

1. **A single train/test split is a coin flip.** The same model on the same dataset can score 5–10 points apart just by changing the random seed. Use CV.
2. **Cross-validation gives mean ± std.** Quote both. "85%" is hiding something; "85% ± 1%" is honest.
3. **The test set is one-shot.** Every time you peek at it during model selection, you contaminate it.
4. **Training error always falls; test error is U-shaped.** The bottom of the U is the model you ship, not the training-error minimum.
5. **High variance means overfitting; high bias means underfitting.** Both errors high → bias. Train low, test high → variance.
6. **Bootstrap doesn't create new data.** It estimates uncertainty in what you have. A biased sample gives you a confident estimate of the biased answer.
7. **A higher score isn't evidence.** Compare against a naive baseline and check whether the gap is statistically significant — not just bigger.
8. **Retrain on all the data after CV.** The model you ship is *not* one of the fold models — it's a final fit on all (train + val) at the chosen configuration.
9. **Distribution drift is silent.** The model didn't get worse — the world changed under it. Monitor in production; retrain when the metric drops.

</CheatRule>

<CheatRule label="One-line summary" accent="navy">
  Pick the metric before you train ([Part 4](/blog/ml-from-scratch-04-classification-metrics)), then <Highlight>validate honestly</Highlight> — cross-validate to get mean ± std, retrain on everything, spend the test set exactly once, and monitor for drift.
</CheatRule>
