*NLP Foundations · Post 2/4*

## What this post is

Handwritten notes from the semantics session. We go from classical word-sense disambiguation into the distributional hypothesis — "you shall know a word by the company it keeps" — and the embeddings that came out of it. Everything you do later with vector similarity, RAG retrieval, and embedding search is downstream of this idea.

## Key takeaways (so you can scan)

- **Distributional hypothesis:** words that appear in similar contexts have similar meanings.
- **Vector embeddings** turn that hypothesis into geometry — similar meaning = small distance in vector space.
- Cosine similarity over normalized embeddings is the operation behind every modern semantic search engine.
