*NLP Foundations · Post 1/4*

## What this post is

These are my handwritten notes from the introductory session of my NLP coursework. We cover what NLP is, the difference between natural and artificial languages, the five levels of linguistic analysis, and the reasons natural language is so much harder than it looks.

## Key takeaways (so you can scan)

- **NLP** is the automatic analysis and understanding of natural language for communication between computers and humans.
- **The five NLP levels:** morphology → syntax → semantics → pragmatics → inference. Each level adds a harder layer.
- Most ML datasets are tiny compared to real-world language. We omit common-sense knowledge and keep ambiguity, which is why pretrained models matter.
